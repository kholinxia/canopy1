Send us an enquiry

Have a question about a plant, want to volunteer as a reviewer, or need help with a submission? Fill in the form below and we'll get back to you. Every field must be filled in, and the form lists anything that is wrong before it is sent. You can also reach this form from the Enquire button on any plant in the Explore pages.

Manual

How to send an enquiry

  1. Fill in your name, email and phone (digits only, up to 10), and your address (postcode is 5 digits).
  2. Choose a plant from the list. The Subject fills itself in. Then add your group size, a reply date and how we should contact you.
  3. Write your question in Comments and tick the consent box.
  4. Press Submit to send, or Cancel to clear the form.
  5. Want to send plant photos? Use Contribute instead.
