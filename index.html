Welcome to Canopy

Why Canopy exists

Malaysia is recognised as one of the world's biodiversity hotspots, but the same forests that shelter thousands of plant species are under growing pressure from agriculture, logging and land conversion. Scientists cannot monitor every hillside and river valley on their own, which is where citizen science comes in. Canopy is an alpha-stage web platform that invites everyday hikers, students and nature enthusiasts to help track Endangered, Rare and Threatened (ERT) plant species across Sarawak.

Through this site, visitors can browse a growing plant encyclopedia organised by family, genus and species, learn how to recognise ERT plants in the field, submit their own sightings for review, and get in touch with the project team. Every contribution — even a single photograph with a location note — adds to a dataset that conservationists can use to prioritise protection efforts.

How the project works

1. Learn

Explore family, genus and species pages to understand what makes a plant rare or threatened.

2. Spot

Use the identification guide when you're out on the trail to narrow down what you've found.

3. Report

Submit your sighting through the contribution form so it can be verified by our reviewers.

Did you know?

Sarawak alone is home to more than 8,000 recorded species of flowering plants, and new species are still being described every year in its interior forests.

Manual

Using Canopy

  1. Use the menu at the top for the main pages. On a phone, tap the Menu button. Video, the two Acknowledgement pages and the two Enhancements pages (CSS and JavaScript) are in the footer.
  2. Start with Explore to learn about families, genera and species.
  3. Out on the trail? Use Identify to narrow down what you found, then Contribute to report it.

Spotted a plant, or have a question?

Send in a sighting for review, or ask the team about the project.
