The Canopy Plant Encyclopedia

Our encyclopedia is organised the same way botanists classify plants: from broad families, down to more specific genera, down to individual species. For this alpha version we are focusing on two families of rainforest trees, Dipterocarpaceae and Burseraceae, which are both important to Borneo's forests and both affected by logging and land clearance.

Where to spot them

These are mostly tall trees of lowland and hill forest, so the best clues are often on the ground: fallen winged fruit, compound leaves and flakes of bark. Dipterocarpus grandiflorus is listed as Endangered across its range, while the tallest Hopea nutans on record was measured in Tawau Hills National Park in Sabah.

Keep exploring

Found something in the forest that you cannot match to a species? The Identify page explains how to narrow it down from the fruit, leaves and bark. To see who first described these plants, visit the Contributor page.

A note on scope

This alpha release covers two families, two genera per family and two species per genus (eight species in total) as a demonstration dataset. Future versions of the platform are intended to expand this to cover the full range of ERT plants found across Sarawak.
