Contribute a sighting

Found a plant you think might be an ERT species? Fill in the form below and one of our reviewers will take a look. Every field must be filled in. If something is wrong, the form lists all the problems together before it is sent.

Tips for a useful submission

Photos taken in good light, with the whole plant visible in at least one shot, are much easier for reviewers to confirm. See the Identify page for a full field checklist.

Manual

How to upload a sighting

  1. Enter your name (letters and spaces, up to 25), your email, phone (digits only, up to 10) and address (postcode is 5 digits).
  2. Enter the plant’s name and how many plants you saw (a number above 0).
  3. Under Photo(s), click Choose files and pick your images. Hold Ctrl (Cmd on Mac) to select several.
  4. Add the location, date and anything unusual in Comments.
  5. Press Submit to send, or Cancel to clear the form.
